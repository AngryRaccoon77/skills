# Default Portfolio Mandate

Use these as default guardrails, not as immutable promises. The user can override them explicitly.

## Strategic allocation

For a 3-5+ year goal and moderate risk:
- Russian equities: target 45%, normal band 35-55%.
- Bonds: target 45%, normal band 35-55%.
- Cash / money-market: target 10%, normal band 5-15%.

Do not force rebalancing when an allocation is only slightly outside a band. Prefer using new contributions to correct drift.

## Equity concentration

Default maximums based on total portfolio value:
- One corporate issuer/group: 10%.
- One equity sector: 25%.
- Very cyclical or sanction-sensitive exposures should normally stay below the general maximum unless diversification elsewhere is strong.

A limit breach is a review trigger, not an automatic sell signal. Correct with future contributions first when practical.

## Bond concentration

- OFZ: no single-issuer limit; manage by duration and maturity bucket instead.
- One corporate issuer/group across bonds and equities combined: normally <=10% of the total portfolio.
- One corporate bond line: normally <=5-7% of the total portfolio.
- Avoid using weak/opaque credit merely to increase yield.

Prefer a mix of maturities and coupon structures when it improves robustness. In a high-rate regime, compare fixed-rate duration risk against floaters and short maturities rather than assuming one structure is always superior.

## Liquidity reserve

Keeping 5-15% in cash or a liquid money-market instrument is acceptable. Increase the reserve temporarily when:
- the user expects a near-term purchase;
- the portfolio is already concentrated;
- available candidates do not offer enough expected return for their risk;
- market liquidity or event risk is unusually poor.

## Goal de-risking glidepath

If the goal amount and date become concrete, protect the money that will actually be needed for the goal.

Suggested default:
- More than 24 months to goal: use the normal strategic allocation.
- 18-24 months: begin reducing the portion of equity risk associated with the goal.
- 12-18 months: hold a majority of the required goal amount in short-duration high-quality bonds / money-market exposure.
- Less than 12 months: do not rely on equity appreciation for money required for the purchase; prioritize capital stability and liquidity.

Apply the glidepath to the amount needed for the goal, not automatically to the entire lifetime investment portfolio.

## Behavior rules

- No leverage, shorts, options, futures, or averaging down without a valid thesis.
- No all-in trades.
- No revenge trading after losses.
- No buying merely because a security is down a lot.
- No selling merely because the market is red.
- No forced investment of idle cash.
- Prefer staged contributions and portfolio fit over market-timing bets.
